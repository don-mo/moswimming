# MoSwimming — Photo & Likeness Release

**WHAT THIS IS:** A one-page form a parent signs before their child's image appears on the MoSwimming website, social media, or any marketing materials. Have a lawyer review before you use it. This is a checklist/template, not legal advice.

---

## WHAT THE FORM MUST INCLUDE (checklist)

- [ ] **Child's full name** and age
- [ ] **Parent/guardian full name** (the child is a minor — only the parent/guardian signature has legal weight)
- [ ] **Clear statement of consent:** "I grant MoSwimming permission to photograph and/or video my child and to use those images for the purposes listed below."
- [ ] **Scope of use** — list explicitly:
  - MoSwimming website (moswimming.com)
  - Instagram / Facebook / other social media
  - Printed marketing materials (flyers, etc.)
- [ ] **Statement of no payment:** "I understand there is no compensation for this use."
- [ ] **Exclusions / limits** (optional but kind): "No last names, home locations, or school names will appear alongside my child's image."
- [ ] **Revocation clause:** "I may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing donmo99@gmail.com. Existing uses that have already been published may not be immediately removable, but no new uses will occur after revocation."
- [ ] **Duration:** "This consent is valid until revoked in writing."
- [ ] **Parent/guardian signature** with date
- [ ] **Your (MoSwimming) signature** as the receiving party

## DRAFT TEMPLATE (fill in the blanks)

```
PHOTO & LIKENESS RELEASE — MoSwimming

This consent remains in effect until revoked in writing.

```

## PRACTICAL NOTES

**When to collect:** Before taking any photos. Ideally at enrollment, not on photo day.

**Renewal:** Collect a fresh form at the start of each new "season" or year. Circumstances change.

**Photo-shy kids:** Ask each child before photographing them: "Hey, is it okay if I get a photo of you swimming? You can totally say no." If they say no, they don't appear — regardless of what the release says. The parent's legal consent and the child's in-the-moment comfort are separate things.

**Keep the signed forms:** Physical or scanned digital copies, stored somewhere safe. If a parent later disputes consent, you need the paper.

**SVG/vector files note (for the logo):** This form covers photos only. Your logo vectorization is a separate agreement with your designer — make sure you own the final SVG/AI files and have a written agreement saying so.
