## Shot tips

- **Volume:** Shoot 100–150 per session, keep the best 1–2 per slot. Free to shoot generously on a phone.
- **Announce it:** "We're taking photos for the website on [date], releases will be signed." Known + consented = fine.
- **Light:** Aim for overcast daylight or window-lit indoor pool. Avoid directly pointing into the overhead lights (pool blue blows out easily — see below).
- **Blown-out pool blue:** If the water looks like a flat glowing rectangle instead of real water, your exposure is too hot. Drop your exposure 1–2 stops and reshoot.
- **What to avoid:** Close-ups of torsos, anything that isolates body parts, anything that could be cropped out of context. The rule: "Would every parent be delighted to see this public?" If you feel a flicker of doubt, skip it.
- **After shooting:** AI batch color-correction (Lightroom's AI Auto or Luminar) is fine for matching color temperature across a session. Export at 1200–1800px wide, compress to under 200kb each (use squoosh.app or Next.js Image optimization handles it automatically).
- **Clothing:** Encourage rash guards / swim shirts for any kid whose family prefers more coverage — offer the option before photo day.
